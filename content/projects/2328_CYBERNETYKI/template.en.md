A complex consisting of two residential buildings with commercial premises, complementing one of the first residential quarters on Cybernetyki Street /also designed by our studio/ in Warsaw's "Mordor" district. The primary objective was to create a welcoming urban living space for both residents and neighbors. The entire estate forms a green, open space accessible to everyone.
